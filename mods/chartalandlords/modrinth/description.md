# ChartaLandlords

**Doudizhu (Fight the Landlord) on [Charta](https://modrinth.com/mod/charta)'s card tables.**
3 players (54 cards) or 4 players (double deck). Place a card table, sit down with friends — or leash a mob
onto a chair — and pick *Doudizhu* from the table's game list.

**Requires [Charta](https://modrinth.com/mod/charta) 1.2.5+** (NeoForge 1.21.1).

* Grab the landlord (default) or classic 1/2/3 bidding; reveal / double stakes (×2 each, ×4 stacked).
* Complete rules — straights, double straights, airplanes, four-with-two, bombs and the rocket.
* Scoring with bomb / spring multipliers plus a cumulative scoreboard, a card tracker and a hands viewer.
* Three AI levels (cautious / balanced / aggressive). The in-game `?` screen has the full rules.

---

**在 [Charta](https://modrinth.com/mod/charta) 的牌桌上打斗地主。** 3 人局 54 张，4 人局 108 张两副牌：
摆一张牌桌，坐下（对手可以是真人，也可以把生物拉到椅子上当 AI），右键牌桌选「斗地主」。
**需要 Charta 1.2.5+**（NeoForge 1.21.1）。

* 抢地主（默认）或经典叫分；明牌 / 加倍（各 ×2，叠满 ×4）；结算是逐对的，永远零和。
* 完整牌型与各家口径不一的边界规则、记牌器、看牌界面、三档 AI 难度。
* 界面左上角 `?`（玩法）里有完整规则、全部牌型与计分说明，中英双语。
